anon@tide-desk ~ % man tide

the fine print.

What's live, what's paper, and what it will never ask you for.

is this financial advice?

No. TIDE shows how a narrative is moving. It never tells you to buy or sell, and it never will.

what's live and what's paper?

Index levels are live, built from DexScreener and GeckoTerminal prices. The launch radar is live from the pump.fun stream. Positions are paper in the beta: they track the live index, no SOL moves, there is nothing to sign.

how is a tide built?

Solana coins whose name or ticker matches the narrative, found through DexScreener and GeckoTerminal search and GeckoTerminal's trending list. A coin must be a day old, with $8k+ liquidity (or $15k+ market cap on the pump.fun curve), $5k+ volume and 50+ trades in 24h. The 20 most traded go in, equal weight. Every tide starts at 1,000.

can one coin pump a whole tide?

No. Each coin's move counts up to ±50%, and a tide needs 5 coins to open for trading. With 20 inside, one coin moves the tide 2.5 points at most. Coins on their first day can't join, and fake-volume clones with almost no trades are filtered out.

who pays the winners?

The losing side of the same tide, through one pool. The treasury never takes the other side. 40% of fees fill an insurance pool for when one side is much bigger than the other, and a win pays at most your collateral again.

what can't it do?

A keyword basket is a blunt tool: a coin can match a narrative by name without belonging to it. Thin tides are marked thin. DexScreener data can lag by a minute.

do I need a wallet?

Not to read the tides or paper trade. Never paste a seed phrase or private key anywhere, here included.

will tide DM me?

Never. Anyone who does is not us.
